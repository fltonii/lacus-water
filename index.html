Lacus Water

Tap once per refill.

Lacus is a simple water tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch. Pick the bottle you actually drink from, and your daily goal becomes a number of refills. Log with one tap in the app, from a widget, Control Center, Siri, a reminder or your Apple Watch.

Help

How is my goal calculated?
About 33 ml per kilogram of body weight, plus extra for activity and hot weather, rounded up to whole containers. You can set your own goal in Settings.
How do reminders work?
Your goal is paced to finish a couple of hours before bed. Lacus checks in after a quiet stretch, nudges you every hour if you fall a container behind, sends a catch-up and a last call in the evening, and stays quiet once you reach your goal. Adjust it in Settings › Reminders.
How do I delete a log?
Swipe left on it in the Today list, or long-press it.
Why does a late drink count for yesterday?
Your day starts at 4 AM, so a glass after midnight still counts for the evening it belongs to. You can change this in Settings.
Will my data move to a new iPhone?
Yes, when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start.
